# Guide: Negotiation and Pushback

FDEs negotiate all the time: a customer asks for more than fits, and you have to
trade scope, timeline, and quality without losing their trust.

**What great looks like**
1. **Clarify the real need** — what problem the ask solves, who needs it, and
   by when, before you agree or refuse.
2. **Decompose the ask** — split it into must-have and nice-to-have, and offer
   a smaller first deliverable that meets the deadline.
3. **Name the risk of saying yes** — what breaks if you promise all of it
   (quality, a missed date, the team), backed by evidence.
4. **Communicate the trade-off** — "X by Friday, or X and Y in three weeks:
   which matters more?" Then confirm the decision in writing.

**Anti-patterns:** a flat "no" with no alternative; quietly agreeing and then
missing the date; cutting quality without telling anyone.
